Joyce Yang

4th year Computer Science undergraduate student @ Cornell University.

I work in the EmPRISE Lab with Prof. Tapomayukh Bhattacharjee on physical human-robot interaction and human-in-the-loop frameworks. I also worked with Prof. Somil Bansal in Stanford’s Safe and Intelligent Autonomy (SIA) Lab on safety filtering for end-to-end visuomotor policies.

Previously, I worked on reinforcement learning for in-hand manipulation of bioinspired robots through the Science Internship Program at UC Santa Cruz, under the mentorship of Dr. Michael Wehner. I also interned at Stanford University’s Robinson Lab, where I studied gene expression in equine RNA-seq data to better understand cartilage degradation.

  2. Brick by Brick: Using Collaborative Robotics to Combat Social Isolation in Public Spaces
    Joyce Yang, Phillip Johnson, Nyra Graham, and Karen Shamir
    In Companion Proceedings of the 21st ACM/IEEE International Conference on Human-Robot Interaction, Edinburgh, Scotland, UK, 2026
    Abs DOI

    Social isolation in shared spaces threatens community cohesion and well-being. This paper presents a social robot designed to spark human-to-human interactions. Inspired by public art projects, the robot invites individuals to collaborate on a shared LEGO structure by using expressive eye tracking, autonomous turning, and servo-actuated drawer movement. Field deployments in Cornell University spaces showed the robot effectively acted as a social catalyst: diverse participants contributed to a shared structure, and strangers initiated conversations about the robot. This work offers a functional prototype and insights on robots as mediators of human connection and promotes ideas of empowering collaboration.
